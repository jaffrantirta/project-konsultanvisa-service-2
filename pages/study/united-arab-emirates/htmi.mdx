# HTMi - Hotel and Tourism Management Institute Dubai

## Why Study in Dubai?

- 🌍 Dubai is one of the most cosmopolitan cities in the world, home to nearly 200 nationalities.  
- 🏨 Global hub for hospitality brands like **Marriott, Hilton, Accor, Jumeirah, Atlantis, Ritz Carlton, and Four Seasons**.  
- 🌐 4th most visited city globally in 2019 with **16.73 million tourists**.  
- 🎢 Home to famous attractions: **Warner Brothers, IMG World, Ferrari World, Atlantis Waterpark**.  
- 😊 Consistently ranks as one of the happiest countries in the Arab world.  
- 🎉 Major international events: **Dubai World Cup (horse racing), DP World Tour Championship (golf), Expo 2020 Dubai**.  
- 💼 Excellent opportunities for **hospitality internships and careers** in a fast-growing tourism market.  
- 🎓 **HTMi Dubai** offers Swiss-certified programs with real-world training in hotel rooms, kitchens, event spaces, and hospitality venues.

## 📌 Requirements Study in HTMi Dubai
- Minimum **17 years old**.  
- Genuine passion for hospitality.  
- **High school certificate** (local/international recognized).  
- English proficiency:  
  - TOEFL **450**, or  
  - IELTS **5.0**, or  
  - EMSAT **1100**, or equivalent.  
- Industry experience or prior education in hospitality considered case-by-case.
