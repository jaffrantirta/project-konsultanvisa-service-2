## Programs & Courses

### STARTER
Focused on essential conversation starters. Emphasis is placed on frequently used verbs, prepositions, and question–answer structures.  
By the end of this level, students can clearly differentiate between **Are you, Do you, Can you, Could you, Would you**, etc.

---

### PRE-FLUENT
Designed for learners with basic understanding of **past, present, and future tenses**.  
Students expand vocabulary through relevant topics and everyday conversations.  
By the end of this level, students become **more confident language users**.

---

### FLUENT
Focused on engaging in **complex discussions and debates** through paraphrasing to diversify speech and expand vocabulary.  
By the end of this level, students can engage with the language independently and progress through **books and films**.

---

### INTENSIVE COURSE
Designed for **rapid learning** and improvement of speaking skills in a short period.  
The course focuses on job-specific phrases and vocabulary.  
With a fast-paced curriculum, students boost **confidence and fluency within clear deadlines**.
