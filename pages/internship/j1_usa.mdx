# J-1 Internship Program USA

The J-1 Exchange Visitor Program is a non-immigrant visa program that promotes educational and cultural exchange between the USA and other nations. **Konsultan Visa** partners with multiple designated sponsors to offer paid hospitality internship and trainee opportunities across the United States.

---

## 🧾 General Eligibility

### J1 Interns
- Must be a **current student** or **recent graduate (within 12 months)** from a post-secondary institution (offline/online)
- Eligible programs: Vocational, Diploma (D1/D2/D3/D4), S1, or S2

### J1 Trainees
- Graduated **more than 12 months ago** and:
  - Have **1+ year** of relevant work experience outside the USA, **or**
  - Have **5+ years** of total work experience outside the USA
- Must have **1–5 years** of hotel experience in the **same role applied for**

> **Note**: If not graduated from a hospitality school, you must have **at least 5 years** of experience in the same position.

---

## ✅ Program Requirements

- **Age**: 18–35 years old
- **J1 Interns**: Minimum **6 months** of hospitality work experience
- **J1 Trainees**: **1–5 years** of hotel experience (same position) or recent graduate (within the last 5 years)
- **Education**: Minimum **6 months full-time study** in a hospitality program
- **English**: Good conversational skills
- **Passport**: Valid for **6+ months** beyond program end date
- **Graduation Timeline**: Recent graduates must have finished **within the past 12 months** (for Interns)

### Education Requirements by Background

| Education Background | Required Hospitality Work Experience |
|---------------------|--------------------------------------|
| SMK/SMA Graduate | Minimum 5 years |
| Hospitality D1 Graduate | Minimum 1 year |
| Non-Hospitality S1/S2 Graduate | Minimum 1 years |

---

## 💵 Program Benefits

- **Salary**: USD **$8–$20/hour** (varies by sponsor and location)
- **Tips/Service Charge**: Shared tips in addition to hourly wage
- **Working Hours**: 30–40 hours per week (minimum 32 hours guaranteed)
- **Housing & Transportation**: May be provided depending on company policy
- **Experience**: Cultural exchange, career training & international exposure
- **Grace Period**: 30-day travel period after program ends (no work allowed)

---

## ⏳ Program Duration

- **6 to 12 months** (1 year typical)

---

## 📌 Available Positions

- **Front Office**
- **Food & Beverage (F&B) Service**
- **Culinary / Food Production**

> **Note**: No advanced training positions available. 2nd-time J1 applicants must apply for a different position with relevant experience.

---

## 🎯 Program Objectives

- Enhance mutual understanding between people of the U.S. and your country
- Participate in cross-cultural activities with Americans
- Return home to share your U.S. experience and knowledge

---

## 📑 Required Documents

Submit all as **digital copies**. You **must have a passport** to start the process.

- Valid passport (color copy)
- National ID card
- Academic certificates and transcripts
- Employment letters (current & previous, on company letterhead)
- Professional photo
- Bank statement showing minimum USD $10,000

---

## ⚠️ Important Notes

- **Cultural Activities**: Participation in cross-cultural activities is mandatory
- **Non-Immigrant Intent**: You must intend to return home after the program
- **Grace Period**: 30 days after program end for travel only (not work)
- **Visa Rule 212(e)**: Some participants must return home for 2 years before applying for certain U.S. visas (H, L, K, immigrant)
- **Extensions**: Possible on a case-by-case basis (fees apply)
- **Repeat Participation**: Must wait 2 years before reapplying; must show more advanced training objectives
