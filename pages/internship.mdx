## 📋 General Application Process

1. **Submit Your Application**: Send your CV to **admin@konsultanvisa.com**
   - Subject: `INTERNSHIP_FULLNAME_PROGRAM` (e.g., INTERNSHIP_JOHNDOE_USA)

2. **Pre-Screening Interview**: English proficiency and experience eligibility assessment

3. **Document Preparation**: Prepare passport, certificates, transcripts, employment letters, etc.

4. **Sponsor/Provider Interview**: Interview with placement provider or sponsor organization

5. **Host Company Interview**: Final interview with the hotel/company

6. **Visa Application**: Complete visa process and prepare for departure

---

## ✅ General Eligibility

- **Age**: 18-35 years old (varies by program)
- **Education**: Minimum Diploma (D3) in hospitality or related fields
- **Experience**: 6 months to 2 years hospitality work experience (varies by program)
- **English**: Good conversational skills (IELTS 5.5+ for Australia)
- **Passport**: Valid 6+ months beyond program end date
